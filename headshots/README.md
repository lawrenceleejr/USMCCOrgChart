Headshots go here, one per person, named after their `id` in
`config/chart.yaml` — e.g. `holmes.jpg` for `id: holmes`.

Accepted extensions: `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp`. Images are cropped to
a circle from the centre, so square-ish crops work best; roughly 400×400 or
larger keeps the print-scale PNG sharp. A person with no file here renders as
an initials placeholder.
